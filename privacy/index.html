Privacy note

What we collect through this website, and why.

What we collect. Only what you type into our forms: your name, phone, optional email, ZIP code, the kind of care, job or referral you’re asking about, and an optional message.

Why. To call you back about care, jobs or referrals with CareLife 365. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

Who can see it. Our team. Form messages are delivered through our website host, Netlify.

Please don’t send medical information. Leave out diagnoses, Medicaid ID numbers, Social Security numbers and medical records. We’ll collect what’s needed privately once care is being arranged.

Removing your information. Ask us when we call, and we’ll delete it.

This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies.
