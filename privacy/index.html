Privacy note

What we collect. Only what you type into the “Request care” form: your name, phone, optional email, ZIP code, the kind of care or job you’re asking about, and an optional message.

Why. To call you back about care or work with KairoBridge Home Care. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

Who can see it. Our team. Form messages are delivered through our website host, Netlify.

Please don’t send medical information. Leave out diagnoses, Medicaid ID numbers, Social Security numbers and medical records. We’ll collect what’s needed privately once care is being arranged.

Removing your request. Ask us when we call, and we’ll delete it.

This website doesn’t use analytics or advertising cookies.
