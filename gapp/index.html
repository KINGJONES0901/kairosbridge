GAPP home nursing for children

The Georgia Pediatric Program (GAPP) is a Georgia Medicaid program that pays for nursing and personal care at home for medically fragile children.

Target program · participation pending

Who GAPP is for

  • Children and young adults under 21
  • Eligible for Georgia Medicaid
  • Medically fragile and needing skilled nursing and/or personal care that is medically necessary

The state makes the final decision on eligibility and approved hours. Program rules change, so we check current requirements with each family.

What care can include

  • Skilled nursing at home by RNs and LPNs, often called private duty nursing
  • Personal care support with bathing, dressing, feeding and moving safely

How to get started

  1. Talk with your child’s doctor. GAPP care starts with a physician’s order.
  2. Choose a GAPP agency. Families pick a Medicaid-enrolled GAPP provider.
  3. The agency requests approval. It gathers the orders and submits the prior authorization request.
  4. Care begins. A nurse builds the care plan and staff start the approved hours.

CareLife 365 is preparing to become a GAPP provider. Enrollment isn’t complete yet, so we can’t submit GAPP requests today. Send us a request and we’ll follow up when we can.

Common questions

Does my family pay?

For approved GAPP services, Medicaid pays the agency.

Is GAPP the same as ICWP?

No. GAPP is for children under 21. ICWP is for adults 21 to 64.

Can I change agencies?

Families choose their GAPP provider and can ask to change. Your physician and the program will guide the process.
