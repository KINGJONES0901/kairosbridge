In-home personal care

Help with the routines of daily life, delivered with patience and respect for privacy.

Planned

What personal care includes

  • Bathing, grooming and dressing
  • Toileting and incontinence care
  • Meals and help with eating
  • Moving, transfers and walking safely

Aides work within their training, under a care plan overseen by a registered nurse.

Who pays

Personal care can be covered through GAPP (as personal care support for children) and ICWP (as personal support for adults), when approved.
