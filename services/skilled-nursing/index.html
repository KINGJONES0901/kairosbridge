In-home skilled nursing

RN and LPN care at home for children and adults with complex medical needs, following the physician’s orders.

Planned

What our nurses will do

  • Scheduled nursing shifts at home (private duty nursing)
  • Medications, tube feedings and respiratory care, as ordered
  • Monitoring, notes and updates to the family and doctor
  • Care plans written and supervised by a registered nurse

Each nurse works within their license and training, and the plan follows the physician’s orders.

Who pays

We focus on Georgia Medicaid: GAPP for children under 21 and ICWP for adults 21 to 64.

Who it’s for

Children and adults who need skilled care at home, for example after a hospital stay or with a long-term condition.
