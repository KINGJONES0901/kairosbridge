TARGET PROGRAMS · PARTICIPATION PENDING

GAPP Georgia Pediatric Program

Target program · participation pending
Who it serves
Medicaid-eligible children under 21 who are medically fragile and need skilled nursing and/or personal care support.
Care at home
In-home skilled nursing (RN and LPN) and personal care support, based on the approved care plan.
How it starts
The child’s physician orders care, and a Medicaid-approved GAPP agency submits the request for prior authorization.

ICWP Independent Care Waiver Program

Target program · participation pending
Who it serves
Medicaid-eligible adults aged 21 to 64 at application with severe physical disabilities or a traumatic brain injury.
Care at home
Personal support with daily living and nursing support, as set out in the person’s service plan.
How it starts
The person applies to ICWP through the state. Once approved, a case manager helps build the service plan and choose providers.

Eligibility, medical necessity and authorization are decided by the program, not by our agency. Program rules change; we confirm current requirements with each family.

Services we are preparing to provide

Skilled care and everyday support, in one plan.

Final availability depends on licensure, qualified staffing, the care assessment and program authorization.

Skilled nursing

Planned

RN and LPN care at home for children and adults with complex medical needs, following the physician’s orders and care plan.

  • Private duty nursing shifts
  • Medication, feeding and respiratory support within orders
  • Clinical notes and family updates

Personal care support

Planned

Help with the routines of daily living, delivered with patience and respect for privacy.

  • Bathing, grooming and dressing
  • Meals and eating assistance
  • Mobility and transfers within staff competency

Care coordination

Planned

One point of contact working with the family, physician and case manager so the plan stays current.

  • Physician orders and authorization paperwork
  • Scheduling and staff matching
  • Plan reviews when needs change

How it works

From first call to first visit.

This is the process we are preparing. It begins once we are licensed and enrolled.

  1. Talk with us

    Tell us who needs care, where you live and which program applies.

  2. Confirm the program

    We check GAPP or ICWP status, physician orders and what authorization is needed.

  3. Nurse assessment

    A registered nurse assesses needs at home and drafts the care plan.

  4. Start care

    Once authorized, qualified staff begin visits with one point of contact for the family.

Questions

Before we open.

Are you accepting clients now?

No. KairoBridge is in development and is not yet accepting clients, providing care, or collecting patient information.

Are you licensed or enrolled with Medicaid?

Licensure and payer participation are planned launch requirements. This website does not represent that approvals have been obtained.

Do you take private insurance or Medicare?

Our focus is Georgia Medicaid home care through GAPP and ICWP. We do not plan to bill Medicare. Any other payment options will be listed here only once they are in place.

Where will services be available?

Our proposed starting area is metro Atlanta. Final counties and service availability will be confirmed through the licensing and staffing process.

Will care cost my family anything?

For approved GAPP or ICWP services, the program pays the agency. Coverage depends on eligibility, medical necessity and authorization, so we cannot confirm a family’s cost before those are reviewed.

Can nurses and caregivers apply?

A recruiting contact and application process will be added before opening. No job applications or personal documents are collected through this preview.
