How to refer

  1. Ask us to call. Use the form with your contact details only.
  2. Secure handoff. We take patient details by phone, never through this website.
  3. Fast follow-up. We confirm staffing, then work with you on orders and authorization.

Ask us to call

Please don’t include the patient’s name, date of birth, Medicaid ID or health details. We’ll call you and take those over a secure channel.
