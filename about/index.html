About KairoBridge

A locally owned metro Atlanta home care agency focused on families who rely on Georgia Medicaid.

Why we exist

Families caring for a medically fragile child or an adult with a disability should be able to get skilled, reliable care at home. We’re building an agency around that: nurses and aides who show up, one contact who answers, and care plans that follow the person.

What we focus on

  • Skilled nursing (RN and LPN) and personal care at home
  • Georgia Medicaid programs: GAPP for children, ICWP for adults
  • Metro Atlanta
