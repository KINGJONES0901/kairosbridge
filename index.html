The care we are building

Support for everyday life.
Attention to what matters.

Our planned services bring together clinical support and help with daily routines. Final availability will depend on licensure, qualified staffing, the care assessment, and applicable payer approval.

01 / CLINICAL CARE

In-home nursing

Planned

Planned nursing support from appropriately licensed professionals, guided by the individual’s care plan and clinical needs.

  • RN and LPN care
  • Care coordination with the clinical team
  • Documentation and family communication
02 / DAILY ROUTINES

Personal care

Planned

Planned assistance that respects privacy, preferences, and each person’s daily routine.

  • Bathing, grooming, and dressing
  • Eating and daily living assistance
  • Mobility and transfer assistance within staff competency
03 / FAMILY CONNECTION

Coordinated support

Planned

A planned approach that helps families understand the next step and who is responsible for their care.

  • Individual service planning
  • Scheduling and caregiver communication
  • Review of changing support needs

TARGET PROGRAMS · PARTICIPATION PENDING

Georgia Pediatric Program · GAPP

Target program · participation pending

GAPP supports eligible medically fragile children with medically necessary in-home nursing and/or personal care. Services depend on eligibility, medical necessity, and authorization.

Independent Care Waiver Program · ICWP

Target program · participation pending

ICWP supports eligible adults with severe physical disabilities or traumatic brain injury. It is a separate program with its own eligibility and provider requirements.

Our planned approach

Start by listening.
Build around the person.

Our guiding priorities are dignity, clear communication, and care matched to the individual. This is the experience we are preparing to deliver.

  1. Understand the family’s needs

    Discuss goals, location, daily routines, and the support being sought.

  2. Confirm the right care pathway

    Review service scope, clinical assessment, and any required eligibility or authorization.

  3. Plan and coordinate care

    Once operational, arrange an agreed service plan, appropriately qualified staff, and a reliable point of contact.

Before we open

Your questions, answered.

Are you accepting clients now?

No. KairoBridge is in development and is not yet accepting clients, providing care, or collecting patient information.

Are you licensed or enrolled with Medicaid?

Licensure and payer participation are planned launch requirements. This website does not represent that approvals have been obtained.

Where will services be available?

Our proposed starting area is metro Atlanta. Final counties and service availability will be confirmed through the licensing and staffing process.

Will services be free to families?

Payment depends on eligibility, authorized services, payer participation, and the applicable agreement. We cannot confirm coverage or promise a family’s cost before those details are reviewed.

Can nurses and caregivers apply?

A recruiting contact and application process will be added before opening. No job applications or personal documents are collected through this preview.
