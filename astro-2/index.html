I

The care

Three kinds of help, planned around one person.

What we are preparing to offer. Final availability depends on licensure, qualified staffing, the nurse’s assessment and program authorization.

01

Skilled nursing

Planned

RN and LPN nurses at the bedside for children and adults with complex medical needs: medications, feeding, respiratory and other care within the physician’s orders.

02

Personal care

Planned

Bathing, dressing, grooming, meals and safe movement around the home, delivered with patience and privacy, within each aide’s training.

03

Care coordination

Planned

One person who keeps the physician, case manager, nurses and family on the same page, from orders and authorizations to the weekly schedule.

“Families caring for a medically fragile child or an adult with a disability should not have to choose between the right care and their own home.”

II

The programs

How care is paid for.

We are preparing to serve families through Georgia Medicaid’s GAPP and ICWP programs. Enrollment and contracting are not yet complete.

TARGET PROGRAMS · PARTICIPATION PENDING

GAPP

Georgia Pediatric Program

Target program · participation pending

For Medicaid-eligible children under 21 who are medically fragile and need skilled nursing and/or personal care support. Care starts with the child’s physician and a prior-authorization request from a Medicaid-approved GAPP agency.

Georgia Medicaid resources (opens in a new tab) ↗

ICWP

Independent Care Waiver Program

Target program · participation pending

For Medicaid-eligible adults aged 21 to 64 at application with severe physical disabilities or a traumatic brain injury. After the state approves an application, a case manager builds the service plan with the person and their chosen providers.

III

The process

What happens, in order.

The path we are preparing. It begins once we are licensed and enrolled.

  1. A conversation about who needs care, where, and which program applies.

  2. The paperwork: program status, physician orders and the authorization request.

  3. A nurse visit to assess needs at home and write the care plan.

  4. Care begins with qualified staff and one person the family can always reach.

IV

Questions

Asked before we open.

Are you accepting clients now?

No. KairoBridge is in development and is not yet accepting clients, providing care, or collecting patient information.

Are you licensed or enrolled with Medicaid?

Licensure and payer participation are planned launch requirements. This website does not represent that approvals have been obtained.

Do you take private insurance or Medicare?

Our focus is Georgia Medicaid home care through GAPP and ICWP. We do not plan to bill Medicare. Any other payment options will be listed here only once they are in place.

Where will services be available?

Our proposed starting area is metro Atlanta. Final counties and service availability will be confirmed through the licensing and staffing process.

Will care cost my family anything?

For approved GAPP or ICWP services, the program pays the agency. Coverage depends on eligibility, medical necessity and authorization, so we cannot confirm a family’s cost before those are reviewed.

Can nurses and caregivers apply?

A recruiting contact and application process will be added before opening. No job applications or personal documents are collected through this preview.
