ICWP home care for adults

The Independent Care Waiver Program (ICWP) is a Georgia Medicaid waiver that helps adults with severe physical disabilities or a traumatic brain injury live at home instead of in a facility.

Target program · participation pending

Who ICWP is for

  • Adults aged 21 to 64 when they apply and start services
  • A severe physical disability or traumatic brain injury
  • Eligible for Georgia Medicaid, with care needs the program approves

People already in ICWP before age 65 can generally keep their services after 65. The state decides eligibility and the service plan.

What care can include

  • Personal support with bathing, dressing, meals, mobility and daily routines
  • Home health services, including nursing, when they’re in the service plan

ICWP also covers things outside our services, such as equipment, home changes and emergency response systems.

How to apply

  1. Apply to ICWP. Start with Georgia’s official application page.
  2. Assessment. The program reviews medical and daily-living needs.
  3. Case manager and plan. Once approved, a case manager builds the service plan with you.
  4. Choose providers. You pick the agencies that deliver your care.

Common questions

Does ICWP cost anything?

Approved ICWP services are paid by Medicaid. The program will tell you about any costs that apply to you.

What about people over 65?

New applicants must be 21 to 64. Older adults may qualify for other Georgia waivers.

What about children?

Children under 21 may qualify for GAPP.

Questions about care at home?

Send a request. We’re opening soon and will call you back.
